WHO WE ARE

About PropertyScope Communications Ltd. (TPS)

PropertyScope Communications Ltd. (The PropertyScope) is a leading real estate media, marketing, and research platform dedicated to promoting transparency, professionalism, and sustainable growth in the real estate sector. Founded by a team of experienced journalists and communication professionals with a strong background in financial journalism and marketing communications, TPS is driven by a passion for ethical, credible, and solutions-oriented reporting.

At The PropertyScope (TPS), we believe that access to reliable information is essential for informed investment and responsible property development. Through quality journalism, market intelligence, property marketing, research, and stakeholder engagement, we connect investors, developers, professionals, policymakers, and the public with the knowledge and opportunities that drive growth in the real estate industry.

Our office is situated in Kano, Nigeria's historic commercial hub. The PropertyScope is committed to showcasing investment opportunities, promoting ethical business practices, supporting evidence-based policies, and positioning Kano as a premier destination for real estate investment. Our vision extends beyond publishing — we aspire to become the most trusted voice and leading platform for real estate transformation in Nigeria, West Africa, and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a real estate platform for emergence of prosperous and secured investment and profitable returs.

Our Mission

To transform the real estate sector through reliable informations, innovative marketing, publicity and professional excellence.

Our History

Founded by a team of experienced journalists and communication professionals with a strong background in financial journalism and marketing communications, The PropertyScope (TPS) was built on a shared passion for ethical, credible, and solutions-oriented reporting — and has grown into a comprehensive media, marketing, and research platform for Kano's real estate sector.
