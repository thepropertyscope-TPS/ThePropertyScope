WHO WE ARE

Nigeria's Trusted Voice for Real Estate Transformation

The PropertyScope Communications Ltd. (TPS) is a leading real estate media, marketing, and research platform dedicated to promoting transparency, professionalism, and sustainable growth in the real estate sector. Founded by a team of experienced journalists and communication professionals with a strong background in financial journalism and marketing communications, TPS is driven by a passion for ethical, credible, and solutions-oriented reporting.

Based in Kano, Nigeria's historic commercial hub, we are committed to showcasing investment opportunities, promoting ethical business practices, and positioning Kano as a premier destination for real estate investment.
